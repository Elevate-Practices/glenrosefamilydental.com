Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible website.

Book a visit(254) 898-4646

Glen Rose Family Dental wants everyone to be able to use our website and get the care they need, including people with disabilities.

Our standard

We design and test this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content easier to use for people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have mobility, speech or cognitive disabilities.

What we do

  • Text and buttons with strong color contrast, and text that can be enlarged without breaking the page.
  • Descriptions on meaningful images so screen readers can describe them.
  • Clear headings and page structure, so each page is easy to follow with a screen reader.
  • Menus, links and buttons that work with a keyboard and show where you are on the page.
  • Pages that work on phones and tablets, with large tap targets.

Tools from other companies

Some features on this site, such as online booking, chat, reviews, maps and patient forms, are provided by other companies. We choose tools that support accessibility, but we do not control all of their content. If one of them is hard to use, call us and we will help you directly.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this website is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please contact us. Tell us the page and the problem, and we will help you get what you need and work to fix it.

Call (254) 898-4646 or email smile@glenrosefamilydental.com.
