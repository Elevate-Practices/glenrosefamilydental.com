Dr. Destini Rodriguez, DDS

General dentist at Glen Rose Family Dental

Book a visit(254) 898-4646

Dr. Destini Rodriguez grew up in Robstown, Texas. She earned her bachelor's degree in biology at Baylor University in Waco and her Doctor of Dental Surgery at Texas A&M University College of Dentistry in Dallas.

Dr. Rodriguez is passionate about patient education and helping patients feel confident about their smiles.

Outside the office she enjoys time with her family and church community and the great outdoors. She also loves movies, and will quote a flick at any chance she gets.

Taking new patients of all ages.
