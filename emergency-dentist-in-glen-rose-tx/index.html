Glen Rose Family Dental offers toothaches & emergencies at our office on Bluebonnet Street in Glen Rose, Texas, for kids, teens and adults from across Somervell County. Call (254) 898-4646 or book online.

Call us first

If you're in pain, call (254) 898-4646. We keep time open for toothaches and will work to see you the same day we're open.

We can help with

  • Toothaches
  • A broken, chipped or cracked tooth
  • A crown or filling that came off
  • Swelling or a tooth that's loose
  • Cuts or injuries to the gums, lips or tongue

Head injury?

Go to the emergency room or see your doctor first, even if a tooth is hurt too. Then call us.

Ways to pay

We're insurance friendly and will help you use your benefits. We also offer an in-office payment plan with interest-free options, and CareCredit.

Common questions

Can you see me today?

Often, yes. Call as early as you can and we'll find the soonest time.

A crown came off. What should I do?

Keep the crown, call us, and avoid chewing on that side until we can put it back on.

What if you're closed?

We're open Monday through Thursday. Leave a message and we'll call you back. For a medical emergency, call 911.
