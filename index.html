Our town

Proudly Glen Rose.

We live here too. Glen Rose is the Dinosaur Capital of Texas, where real dinosaur tracks sit in the bed of the Paluxy River at Dinosaur Valley State Park, and the limestone courthouse still anchors the square.

It's a town where people know each other, and that's how we run our office: unhurried visits, honest answers and a team that remembers your name.

Questions neighbors ask

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We see kids, teens and adults. Book online or call (254) 898-4646.

Do you take my insurance?

We're insurance friendly and in network with many plans. Call us and we'll check yours and help you get the most from your benefits.

I don't have insurance. What can I do?

Join our membership: two cleanings (in the absence of gum disease), exams, X-rays and free whitening each year, plus 20% off other care, starting at $329 a year.

I'm in pain. Can you see me today?

Call us. We work to see toothaches the same day we're open, Monday through Thursday.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We're closed Friday through Sunday.
