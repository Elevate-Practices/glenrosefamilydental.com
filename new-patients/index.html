We welcome new patients and treat you like family. We see children, teenagers and adults, with care that runs from cleanings to smile makeovers.

Your first visit

Your first visit helps you understand your own dental health and get to know the team who'll care for you. You'll have a full exam with X-rays and photos of your teeth, and the doctor will go over what they find. It's a great time to ask questions, and you'll leave with a plan made for you.

Insurance

We're insurance friendly and in network with many plans. Call (254) 898-4646 and we'll help you check your plan and get the most from your benefits.

Payment options

Spread out the cost of treatment with our in-office payment plan, including interest-free options and lower monthly payments. We'll go over every option before treatment, so there are no surprises. Financing is also available through Cherry and CareCredit.

No insurance?

Our membership plan covers your preventive care and saves you 20% on other treatment.
