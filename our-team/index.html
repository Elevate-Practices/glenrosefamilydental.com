Our team

Esther Gutierrez

Office Manager

In dentistry since 2008, a Glen Rose local since 2019, and the reason the office runs on time.

More about Esther

Esther has been in the dental field since 2008, with deep experience in both front office operations and chairside assisting. As Office Manager she keeps the office running smoothly while supporting the team and making sure patients feel welcomed, valued and well cared for.

With a strong background in dental insurance, patient accounts and treatment coordination, she's dedicated to making every visit as comfortable and seamless as possible. Originally from West Texas, Esther moved to Glen Rose in 2019 and is proud to call this community home.

Outside the office she enjoys time with her husband and children, cheering on her kids, and curling up with a good thriller.

Sarah

Registered Dental Hygienist

A hygienist since 2002 and part of this team since 2016. She gardens, fishes and loves being outdoors.

More about Sarah

Sarah has been a valued member of the Glen Rose Family Dental team since 2016 and a dental hygienist since 2002. She's passionate about teaching patients why oral health matters and helping them keep healthy, beautiful smiles.

She believes in making every visit a positive one and loves bringing laughter into the dental chair. One of her favorite parts of the job is getting to know her patients and building lasting relationships with them.

Outside the office, Sarah enjoys gardening, fishing and spending time outdoors.

Regan Murphy

Registered Dental Hygienist

Grew up in Grandview, rodeo weekends and all, and came home to join the team in 2022.

More about Regan

Regan is a native of the area, having grown up in Grandview, Texas. She and her family were active in rodeo, spending many weekends at the Expo or exploring the surrounding area. After school in Beeville, Texas, Regan returned home in June 2022 and joined the Glen Rose Family Dental team shortly after.

Outside the office she enjoys the outdoors with her husband, her daughter, her dogs and her goats, and loves spontaneous adventures with her family.

Regan believes in treating every patient like family and works to create a welcoming, positive atmosphere. She especially enjoys seeing her patients smile and laugh.

Kadie

Registered Dental Hygienist

A hygienist for over four years, and a proud wife and mom to a fun-loving two-year-old.

More about Kadie

Kadie has been a dental hygienist for over four years and is passionate about helping patients achieve and maintain healthy, beautiful smiles. She enjoys building relationships with her patients and making each visit comfortable and positive.

Outside the office, Kadie is a proud wife and mom to a fun-loving two-year-old who keeps life exciting. She loves time with her family, especially outdoors, and in her spare time enjoys crafting and shopping.

Bailey Norris

Registered Dental Assistant

Trained at Waco Dental Assistant School, from Meridian, Texas, and happiest spending time with her daughter.

More about Bailey

Bailey began her dental assisting career in February 2022 after completing her training at Waco Dental Assistant School. Originally from Meridian, Texas, she continues to call the community home.

Bailey is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable and confident throughout their visits. She enjoys building relationships with patients and contributing to a positive experience for everyone who walks in.

Outside the office, Bailey enjoys quality time with her daughter.

Bridget

Registered Dental Assistant

Thirty years in dentistry with a special interest in cosmetic work. Her five grandkids call her “Honey.”

More about Bridget

With 30 years of experience in dentistry, Bridget brings a wealth of knowledge and a passion for helping patients feel confident in their smiles. She has a special interest in cosmetic dentistry and enjoys being part of the process that helps patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles.

Outside the office, Bridget is a proud mom of three adult daughters and a loving grandmother to five grandchildren, who affectionately call her “Honey.” She treasures spending time with her family and enjoys reading, listening to music, and watching movies in her spare time.

Bridget’s experience, dedication, and love for dentistry make her a wonderful part of the Glen Rose Family Dental team!

Shawn

Shawn has been in the dental field since 2020. She's passionate about compassionate care and creating a warm, welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable and cared for, and she takes pride in making each visit a positive one.

Juana

Front Office
